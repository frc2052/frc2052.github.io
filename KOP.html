2027 FRC Kickoff

January 9, 2027, from 10:30 am to 1:30 pm

  • 11:00 am Broadcast begins
  • 11:45 am Q&A session and Field Tour Videos (Approximately)
  • 12:00 pm Event ends (Approximately)

Kickoff Location: 2425 Long Lake Road, New Brighton, MN 55112


Next Steps:

  • Line up in your vehicle at the Irondale main entrance after the kickoff video concludes (12:00 pm)
  • Stay in your vehicle, moving forward as the line moves, and wait until an event coordinator comes to assist you.
  • An event coordinator will validate your team representative for KOP pickup.
  • Your team representative is required to:
    • Be an adult (18 years or older and graduated from high school)
    • Bring a team roster
    • Present a valid photo ID
    • Be Lead mentor 1 or Lead mentor 2 on the team OR have submitted a surrogate pickup letter. If a team mentor that is not Lead 1 or Lead 2 brings a copy of their roster to show they are a mentor on the team, they may pick up the KOP as well.
    • Sign the checkoff sheet indicating that your team has received the KOP.
  • Adult volunteers will bring the KOPs out and place them by your vehicle (no need to enter the school).
  • We ask that you load the KOP into your vehicle, but we will load it for you if assistance is needed.
