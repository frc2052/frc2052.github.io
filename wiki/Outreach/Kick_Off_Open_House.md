# Open House

Updated 2/16/2026

With the later start to Kick Off Broadcast, KnightKrawler began hosting an open house for families, alumni and supporters to visit our space, reconnect, and learn about the team. The open house runs for 2 hours prior to the Kickoff Broadcast. Attendees can stay in the robotics space to watch the kickoff video stream prior to returning home.
