# Setting Up Google Chat Spaces for Team Collaboration

Updated 11/1/26

## Purpose of Google Chat Spaces \- When/How to Use

In 2026-27, Irondale High School implemented the Safe Team platform for team communication, scheduling and announcements. It lacked the functionality our team required to effectively collaborate on documents, media files, design files, etc. We determined that using Google chat spaces, which depend on school-provided technology to function \- could work to safely enable team members to collaborate online and with Mentors. 

SAFE TEAM: this platform will continue to be used for all team communications, calendar announcements, scheduling, etc. Students are required to enroll as a part of their team registration.

GOOGLE CHAT SPACES: this platform will be set up for subteam collaboration on projects during the season. Students will have their school email added to their subteam Spaces as they are created and deemed relevant to their assignments.

## How to Create a Google Chat Space

The Core Mentor in charge of the subteam should create the space using their Team2052.com email address. Inside the Gmail Inbox, on the left hand side there is a “dialog box” icon that denotes the Google Chat interface. Once that interface opens, on the left menu, Spaces should appear below the Direct Message list.Next to the Spaces header, click on the three dots, and select “create new space”. When the box to create a space opens:

- Name the space, using the naming convention SUBGROUP-descriptor (for example, MTR-ALL, BUILD-Electrical, or PROGRAMMING-Autos)  
- Select “Collaboration” as the Space purpose  
- For account settings, select PRIVATE  
- CHECK the box for allowing external members to join. Everything else can be changed after you set up the space, except this, and it’s very important that you enable this setting as you will be inviting email addresses from outside of team2052.com.

Once the space is created, you can start inviting people to the space. 

- Click on the Space in the left hand menu. When the space opens, click on the space name at the top, and select “Manage Members” at the top. Then clich on the blue \+Add button on the upper right of the screen.   
- The first people you should invite is the Lead Mentor and all other Subeteam mentors. Invite them by entering their [Team2052.com](http://Team2052.com) email addresses if they have them, or their preferred email address if they don’t.  
- Then add the student leaders, using their [Team2052.com](http://Team2052.com) addresses. If they prefer, they can also have their student email addresses added.   
- FInally, add the students on the subteam using their student email addresses. The lead mentor will help you with this if needed.   
- Once you’ve added all the email addresses, go back to the “Manage Members” menu. Review the list of members you’ve invited, and find the Lead Mentor and at least one other Core Mentor and change their Role to Owner. Find the Subtream Captains and Leads and Change their Role to Manager.   
- Now select “Space Settings” from the Manage Members menu.Make the following Changes:  
  - Under Membership:  
    - Who can view members: Members  
    - Who can Manage Members: Owners  
  - Permisssions:  
    - Modify space and board details: members  
    - Use @ all: members  
    - Manage Apps: Managers  
    - Manage Webhooks: Managers  
- Finally, post an opening message to the members of the chat: “Welcome to NAME OF SPACE. We can use it fo (DESCRIPTION), etc. NOTE that we will still use Safe Team for meeting announcements, etc but will use this space for when we need to collaborate on documents inside of Google Suite or use functionality that Safe Team can't support. All team rules and guidelines like YPP and GP still apply. Please let MENTOR NAME know if we need to add anyone.”
